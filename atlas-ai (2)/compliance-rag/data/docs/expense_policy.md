# Corporate Expense & Reimbursement Policy

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope

This policy governs all business expense claims submitted by employees,
contractors, and approved vendors for reimbursement. It applies globally
to all business units unless a local regulatory requirement supersedes it.

## Section 2: General Principles

All expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business purposes, and
properly documented. Employees are expected to exercise the same care
spending company funds as they would spending their own money.

## Section 3: Receipt Requirements

Original itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
For expenses under $25, a credit card statement line item is acceptable
in place of a receipt. Receipts must show the vendor name, date, itemized
charges, and payment method. Handwritten receipts are only accepted when
accompanied by a manager's written confirmation.

## Section 4: Approval Thresholds

### Section 4.1: Standard Expenses
Expenses under $500 require approval from the employee's direct manager only.

### Section 4.2: Expense Approval for Larger Amounts
Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require approval from the direct manager
plus the department head. Expenses over $5,000 require additional approval
from Finance and must be submitted with a business justification memo at
least 5 business days before the expense is incurred, except in documented
emergencies.

## Section 5: Prohibited Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: alcohol at non-client-facing events,
personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, first-class airfare
without prior VP approval, and any expense that could reasonably be
perceived as a conflict of interest.

## Section 6: Travel Expenses

Airfare should be booked through the approved corporate travel portal
whenever possible. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours;
premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager
approval. Hotel spend should not exceed the regional per-diem cap listed
in the Travel Rate Schedule (updated quarterly by Finance).

## Section 7: Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense
being incurred. Reports submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed
except with CFO-level exception approval.

## Section 8: Violations and Escalation

Suspected policy violations, including split transactions used to avoid
approval thresholds, must be reported to the Compliance team via the
internal ethics hotline. Confirmed violations may result in disciplinary
action up to and including termination, per the Employee Conduct Policy.
