# Data Handling & Confidentiality Procedure

## Section 1: Data Classification

All company data is classified into one of three tiers: Public, Internal,
and Restricted. Restricted data includes customer PII, financial records,
unreleased product plans, and employee records.

## Section 2: Handling Restricted Data

Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud
storage under any circumstances. It may only be shared with third parties
under an executed Non-Disclosure Agreement and with written approval from
the Data Protection Officer (DPO).

## Section 3: Data Retention

Customer records are retained for 7 years after the end of the business
relationship, after which they must be securely deleted per the Data
Deletion Runbook. Employee records are retained for the duration of
employment plus 5 years, subject to local labor law minimums.

## Section 4: Incident Reporting

Any suspected data breach, including a lost device containing Restricted
data or an email sent to the wrong recipient, must be reported to the
Security team within 24 hours of discovery via the internal incident
portal. Do not attempt to independently investigate or remediate a
suspected breach before reporting it.

## Section 5: Third-Party Vendors

Vendors that will process Restricted data must complete a security
questionnaire and be approved by the DPO before any data is shared.
Vendor access must be reviewed annually.
